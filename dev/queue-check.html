Queue checks

Runs the real queue code through hundreds of simulated open plays and checks the club's rules after every action: Next up and Group 2 take rested players first and a player just off court only when the rested ones cannot make the group (those off court longest first, and such a group is never fixed), fixed groups never change on their own, groups follow the level rules, nobody is lost or doubled, and no court sits idle while a legal game exists. Run it on the local dev server before pushing a queue change. Nothing is saved.
